# PriceYard Official Reference Document — Repository Control Copy

## Authority
This file records the approved product rules supplied by the owner in the PriceYard Official Reference Document. If any repository summary conflicts with the owner's approved source text, the approved source text controls and the discrepancy must enter change control.

## Product identity
- Product: PriceYard
- Legal owner: NGU TOP PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
- Brand line: PriceYard by NGU TOP PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
- Tagline: Know the market before you buy or sell.

## Core promise
PriceYard helps agricultural traders know the market before they buy or sell by structuring commodity price intelligence, previous prices, market signals, quality readiness, storage suitability, buying zones, market-day movements, selling windows, cost information, confidence and freshness.

## Initial MVP focus
- Commodities: Egusi, Beans, Palm oil
- Markets: Abuja/FCT, Kwali Market, Nasarawa, Benue
- First priority: Egusi intelligence around Kwali Market and Nasarawa
- Responsive web app first; no native mobile app first
- Manual validation before full app build

## Required market record
Each market record must support:
Date; Commodity; Market; Market Day; Time of Day; Commodity Type; Unit/Bag Size; Lowest Price; Highest Price; Average Price; Previous Price/Range; Movement; Market Signal; Quality Signal; Storage Suitability; Buying Zone; Sell-Watch Window; Cost Breakdown/Information; Possible Meaning; Suggested Action; Source 1; Source 2; Confidence Level; Notes; Last Updated.

## Product rules
- Use price ranges where exact price is not confirmed.
- Clearly show confidence and last-updated information.
- Track time-of-day changes where available.
- Track market-day information.
- Treat possible meaning and suggested action as non-guaranteed observations/guidance.
- Do not guarantee profit, future prices, preservation, exact transaction prices, or financial outcomes.
- Do not publicly copy paid-group content; use it as learning material and independently verify publishable data.
- Protect private phone numbers, account numbers, vendor instructions and source identities where appropriate.

## MVP users/access
Admin, Free User, Trial User and Paid User are required for the first app. Reporter workflow is later.
- Trial: full approved access for 14 days.
- Paid/active: full approved access.
- Free/expired/cancelled: limited access.
- Admin controls content, users and subscription status.

## MVP application capabilities
Registration/login; admin controls; commodities; markets; price updates; market signals; quality signals; storage suitability; buying zones; cost breakdown; market day; time-of-day entries; current and previous prices; movement; confidence; last-updated; search/filter; watchlist; outdated warning; FAQ; simple report export; manual subscription status; 14-day trial; user feedback with 1–5 stars; mobile-friendly UI.

## Explicitly deferred / not first
AI prediction; guaranteed trading advice; marketplace; buyer/seller transactions; escrow; logistics; payment gateway/card storage; native mobile app; complex charts; advanced alerts; reporter portal/reputation; verified directory; AI trading decisions; loan advice; profit guarantees; complex average-cost/profit systems; automated PDF reporting; API/data subscription.

## Mandatory disclaimers
**Price disclaimer:** Prices are market estimates and may change based on quality, quantity, negotiation, transport cost, bag size, time of day, and market conditions. PriceYard provides market information only and does not guarantee profit or exact transaction prices.

**Market signal disclaimer:** Market signals are observations based on available market information. They are not guaranteed predictions or financial advice. Users should verify before making major buying, selling, or storage decisions.

**Storage suitability disclaimer:** Storage suitability is based on available market and quality information. It does not guarantee profit, preservation, or future price increase.

## Validation requirement
Before coding, the approved business rule requires a 14-day WhatsApp/manual MVP with targets of 50 reached/joined, 10 active users, 5 willing-to-pay/paying users and at least 2 reliable price sources. Any bypass must be explicitly owner-approved and logged with accepted business risk.
